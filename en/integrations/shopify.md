# Shopify <span class="zd-tag soon">Coming soon</span>

The official Zayono Shopify app is under development.

## In the meantime

You can integrate Zayono manually with Shopify via:

- **Shopify Functions + Webhooks**: build your own custom gateway with one of our [SDKs](/en/sdks/) and a payment-method-extension endpoint.
- **Shopify hosted checkout + redirect**: use Shopify for the basket and Zayono for the final payment via `return_url` / `cancel_url`.
