# PrestaShop <span class="zd-tag soon">Coming soon</span>

The official Zayono PrestaShop module is under development.

## In the meantime

You can integrate Zayono manually with PrestaShop via the [PHP SDK](/en/sdks/php) — build a custom module that calls `$zayono->payments->create()` at payment time and redirects to `checkout_url`.
